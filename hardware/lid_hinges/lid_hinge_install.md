# Installing the hidden lid hinges (foot box)

Hardware: 2x Blum 71B3550 hinge (110°, full overlay, soft-close, screw-on), 2x Blum 175H3100 0 mm in-line plate, 1 lid stay, 1 clear bumper pad.
Tools: 35 mm Forstner bit, drill press or hinge-boring jig (or a drill with a depth stop), square, combination square, awl, #2 Phillips, 1/16" (2 mm) pilot bit, sandpaper.
Screws: #6 x 5/8" (15 mm) flat-head wood screws, 4 per hinge cup and 2 per plate. Nothing longer in the lid, or it can poke through the top.

## 0. Before you start
1. Measure the plywood thickness. If it's 23/32" rather than a true 3/4", the steps don't change, but the 5 mm in step 3 matters even more.
2. Hold a straightedge on the back wall where the lid's back end will slide up past it, from 14" to 25" above the floor. If it bulges, sand about 1/16" off the lid's back end.
3. Round over the lid's top edge on the wall side, 1/16" or a little more (sandpaper is fine). Ease the opposite long edge too.
4. Practice steps 3–6 once on a scrap of the same plywood: bore a cup, hang it on a plate screwed to another scrap and check the swing.

## 1. Mark the hinge positions on the lid
Lay the lid upside down. The wall edge is the long edge that sits against the foot wall.
- Mark two hinge centres **4" in from each end** of the lid. Keep them at least 3" in, to clear the bolt on the box front.
- At each mark, square a line across, then mark a point **22.5 mm (about 7/8") in from the wall edge**. That's the cup centre: 5 mm from the edge to the side of the hole plus the 17.5 mm radius. Check it with a combination square.

## 2. Bore the cups
- **Or on the CNC:** use `lid_hinge_cups.dxf` in this folder (see CNC_JOB.md) (lid upside down, zero on the wall-edge corner, pocket 13 mm deep). Then skip steps 1 and 2.
- 35 mm Forstner bit, **13 mm deep (about 1/2")**. Set a depth stop and test on scrap first. The lid is about 19 mm, so 6 mm of wood stays above the cup.
- Keep the bit square to the face. A drill press or jig is best.

## 3. Screw the hinges into the lid
- Press each hinge cup into its hole with the arm pointing straight across the lid toward the wall edge, square to that edge. Use a square against the arm.
- Mark through the screw holes in the cup's flanges with an awl, drill 1/16" pilots about 3/8" deep, then drive the #6 x 5/8" screws.

## 4. Mark the plate positions on the box
Work on the **inside face of the wall-side panel** (the box side against the foot wall).
- Draw a horizontal line **37 mm (about 1-7/16") below the panel's top edge**. The plate's screws go on this line.
- Mark where each hinge centre will land along the panel. The lid sits 1/16" behind the front panel, so measure **4-1/16" from the back face of the front panel** for the front hinge. For the back hinge, measure 4-1/16" from the back wall, or 4" from the lid's back end plus the 1/16" gap there.
- The plate's two screw holes sit on the 37 mm line, one each side of that mark (the plate is symmetric). Centre the plate on the mark, mark the holes, drill 1/16" pilots, then screw it on.

## 5. Hang the lid
- Clip each hinge onto its plate: hook the front of the hinge arm onto the plate, then press the back down until it clicks. A second pair of hands helps hold the lid.
- Close it slowly and look at the gaps.

## 6. Adjust
Target: **about 1/8" (3 mm) between the lid's back edge and the wall when closed**, a 1/16" gap at each end of the lid, and the lid sitting flat on both panels.
- **Side adjustment:** the screw on the hinge arm nearest the cup. It moves the lid toward or away from the wall (±2 mm). Use it to set the wall gap.
- **Depth adjustment:** the cam at the back of the arm. It pulls the lid down tight onto the panel top.
- **Along the length:** if the plate screws are in slotted holes, loosen them and slide. If not, the end gaps come from where you drilled, so be accurate in step 4.
- Open and close it several times. It should never touch the wall on the way up.

## 7. Bumper and lid stay
- Open the lid fully. It tips back and rests on the foot wall at about 92°, roughly 24–25" above the floor. Stick a clear bumper pad on the wall, or on the lid's top face near the top edge, where they touch.
- Fit the lid stay **with the lid hung**, on the **inside of the box back** (not the box front, because there's a bolt head where the stay would go). Use a stay that holds the lid at any angle, rated for about 30 in-lb. Check that it doesn't bind closed or fully open.
- If the lid won't close all the way with the stay fitted, turn off the soft-close on one hinge: Blum hinges have a small switch on the damper.

## Why these numbers
- **5 mm cup distance:** at 7 mm the lid only clears the wall by about 0.15 mm as it starts to open. Blum's minimum-gap table needs about 0.9 mm. At 5 mm you get about 3 mm.
- **0 mm plate, 37 mm line:** this is Blum's standard setup, and the overlay tables assume it.
- **Lid stays off the wall:** at 90° the lid is 12 mm off the wall (Blum P = 12 mm).
